# Management Report — GRC Reporting Dashboard

**Reporting date:** 23 October 2026  
**Organization:** Fictional Northstar Retail  
**Data classification:** Synthetic portfolio demonstration

## Executive interpretation

The latest synthetic snapshot contains six risks: one Critical, three High, and two Moderate before closed records are excluded; one Moderate risk is marked Closed, leaving five open risks. Four open risks are High or Critical. The sample monthly trend shows four open High/Critical risks at each snapshot date; this indicates no change in the count, not necessarily stable underlying exposure.

Five remediation actions are overdue at the sample reporting date. The overdue list includes one Critical and three High-priority actions plus one Moderate-priority action. Owners should confirm revised dates, blockers and escalation status; overdue counts alone do not establish that remediation is ineffective.

The control inventory contains two Effective, three Partially Effective, one Ineffective and two Not Tested controls. Not Tested is not equivalent to effective. The two sample closed actions with valid dates were completed within the configured 30-day SLA (2/2, or 100%); this small sample is not a statistically meaningful performance baseline.

## Management actions

1. Escalate the overdue Critical privileged-access recertification action and require evidence of completion and independent review.
2. Assign accountable owners and target dates for central logging, backup restoration, and vendor assurance actions; document blockers and risk acceptance where applicable.
3. Prioritize testing of controls marked Not Tested, especially backup restoration and vendor assurance review.
4. Validate evidence references, scope and dates before reporting control effectiveness as a formal conclusion.
5. Reconcile source data and KPI calculations before using this dashboard for operational decisions.
