# Executive Summary — Third-Party Risk Management

**Scenario:** Fictional e-commerce organization and fictional SaaS provider  
**Status:** Simulated due diligence package; no real vendor assessed

## Executive message

This project demonstrates a repeatable third-party risk management lifecycle: vendor classification, inherent risk scoring, evidence-based due diligence, control-gap tracking, residual risk governance, conditional onboarding and ongoing monitoring.

The sample CloudCart Services rating is illustrative. Its inherent risk mean is 3.71/5 under the sample unweighted scoring model, which maps to Critical under the defined sample bands. This is not a verified rating. Residual risk is deliberately left unscored because no vendor controls or evidence have been assessed.

## Key decisions before onboarding

1. Confirm the contracted service, data categories, processing locations, integration and business criticality.
2. Obtain relevant current assurance evidence and validate its scope, period, exceptions and subcontractors.
3. Review identity, privileged access, API scopes, encryption, vulnerability management, logging and incident response.
4. Agree recovery objectives and examine restoration/continuity test evidence.
5. Review contractual security, privacy, incident notification, audit, subcontractor, retention, deletion and exit provisions.
6. Assign owners and due dates to confirmed gaps; require closure evidence for material issues.
7. Obtain approval from the authorized business risk owner before accepting residual risk or granting production access.

## Monitoring model

Use risk-based monitoring, periodic reassessment and event-driven reviews. Escalation triggers include a material incident, assurance lapse, ownership or subprocessor change, new sensitive data, material service change, significant vulnerability, missed recovery objective or overdue critical remediation. Cadence in the sample workpaper is illustrative and must be adapted to policy, contract and regulation.

## Management reporting

Recommended KPIs include: percentage of critical vendors with current reviewed assurance evidence; percentage of vendors with approved risk assessments; overdue high/critical findings; days to remediate material issues; vendors with expired exceptions; percentage of critical vendors with tested recovery evidence; and completion of exit/access-revocation checks.

## Limitations

All vendor details, answers, scores and findings are fictional. No real vendor due diligence or control testing occurred. Do not represent this exercise as a completed assessment of an actual supplier.
