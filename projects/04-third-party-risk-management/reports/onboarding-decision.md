2. Obtain and review current independent assurance evidence, including scope, period, exceptions and subservice organizations.
3. Validate identity/MFA, encryption and key management, logging, vulnerability management, incident response and access review controls.
4. Review resilience evidence, including approved recovery objectives and restoration tests.
5. Complete Legal/Privacy review of data-processing terms, incident notification, audit rights, subprocessors, retention/deletion, AI data use and exit provisions.
6. Record confirmed findings, assign accountable owners and due dates, and define closure evidence for material gaps.
7. Recalculate residual risk only after evaluating the control evidence and treatment plan; obtain documented acceptance from the authorized risk owner if residual risk remains above normal tolerance.
