# Project 01: IT Risk Assessment & Cybersecurity Control Assessment

**Project type:** Simulated GRC case study  
**Role demonstrated:** Junior GRC Analyst / IT Risk Analyst  
**Primary framework:** NIST CSF 2.0 concepts  
**Secondary mapping:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A references

## 1. Business scenario

A fictional cloud-based e-commerce business processes customer information and integrates with payment services. Leadership wants a structured view of technology risk, the status of key cybersecurity controls, the evidence needed to assess those controls, and the remediation actions that should receive priority.

(This is just a simulated scenario created for my learning portfolio demonstration; it is not a real client assessment).

## 2. Objective

Demonstrate an evidence-driven workflow for identifying, scoring, mapping, assessing, treating and reporting IT and cybersecurity risks.

## 3. Scope

- Identity and access management
- Privileged access and authentication

- Vulnerability and patch management
- Security logging and monitoring
- Backup and recovery
- Third-party risk
- Secure development and CI/CD
- Secrets management
- Incident response
- Data classification and handling
- Cloud configuration management

## 5. High-priority risk themes

The illustrative risk model highlights privileged access, privileged-account MFA, network segmentation and secrets management for urgent review. These are preliminary priorities based on imagined or assumed likelihood and impact scores, not verified findings about any real organization.

## 6. Method

1. Define business context and assessment scope.
2. Document assumptions and critical business processes.
3. Write risk statements that connect a condition, threat/event and business impact.
4. Score likelihood and impact using the defined 5×5 model.
5. Map risks to relevant control objectives.
6. Identify the evidence needed to evaluate design and operating effectiveness.
7. Record control gaps and uncertainty transparently.
8. Assign risk treatment actions, owners and target dates.
9. Define closure evidence and validation criteria.
10. Report priority risks and decisions to management.

## 7. What this project demonstrates

- IT risk identification and analysis
- Inherent-risk scoring and prioritization
- Control mapping and framework literacy
- Evidence request and assessment planning
- Gap analysis and remediation tracking
- Risk communication for technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Clear separation between assumptions and validated evidence

## 8. Limitations

No live environment was assessed and no real evidence was supplied. Control statuses are preliminary simulated judgments for demonstration. A real assessment would require stakeholder interviews, technical evidence, documented assessment criteria, validation testing and risk-owner review.
