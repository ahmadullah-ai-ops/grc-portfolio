# Executive Summary — IT Risk Assessment

**Assessment type:** Simulated portfolio case study  
**Environment:** Fictional cloud-based e-commerce business  
**Purpose:** Demonstrate evidence-driven GRC analysis and reporting

## Executive message

This illustrative assessment organizes technology and cybersecurity exposure into a risk register, links risks to control objectives, identifies the evidence needed to evaluate those controls, and proposes prioritized remediation actions.

The results are **hypotheses for a simulated scenario**, not verified findings about a real organization. Before management relies on ratings, a real assessment would require agreed scope and risk criteria, stakeholder confirmation, evidence collection, testing and risk-owner review.

## Priority areas

1. **Privileged access:** Validate that administrative roles are justified, approved and periodically reviewed.
2. **Privileged MFA:** Confirm MFA is technically enforced for all in-scope privileged accounts.
3. **Network segmentation:** Validate separation between production, management and sensitive-data environments.
4. **Secrets management:** Check that secrets are not exposed in source code or pipelines and that rotation and access controls are operating.
5. **Vulnerability remediation:** Define time-bound remediation targets and escalate overdue high-risk findings.

## Recommended management decisions

- Confirm the assessment scope and approve the risk-scoring criteria.
- Assign accountable owners to each critical/high risk.
- Prioritize privileged access, MFA and secrets-management validation.
- Approve remediation timelines and exception/escalation rules.
- Require evidence and validation before closing remediation items.
- Review residual risk with the designated risk owner after treatment.

## Reporting principles

A risk score is a prioritization aid, not a precise prediction of loss. A control status should not be treated as validated without sufficient evidence. Remediation closure requires implementation evidence, validation, residual-risk reassessment and approval.

## Portfolio disclosure

This is a simulated case study for professional skills demonstration. No client engagement, production environment, or real control evidence is claimed.
